# Beezy Honey admin: owner guide

This guide is for you, the owner. It explains the admin in plain words.
Open the admin on your phone at **https://admin.beezyhoney.in**.
Tip: in your phone browser, use "Add to Home screen". Then the admin opens like an app.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Open the admin. Type your email and tap **Send me a login code**.
2. A 6-digit code arrives by email from `login@beezyhoney.in`. Check spam the first time.
3. Type the code. It works for 10 minutes and only once.
4. The first time, the admin shows **8 recovery codes**. Print them or take a screenshot. Keep them safe.
   Each recovery code can replace an email code once, if the email does not arrive.
5. You stay logged in on that phone for 7 days.

Only the two owner emails can log in. To make new recovery codes, or to log out on every phone and computer,
go to **Help → Your login** (also on the Backup screen).
- New recovery codes: if you logged in more than 10 minutes ago, tap **Email me a code** first and type that code.
  You also get an email each time new codes are made. If you did not make them, log out on all devices and make new ones.
- **Log out on all devices** also stops any login code that was not used yet. Your recovery codes keep working, so
  if someone else may have them, make new ones too.

## 2. Every day

### When a WhatsApp order arrives
1. The message says "Order BZ-7K3QD" (the code changes each time).
2. Open the admin. On **Today**, type the code in the find box. The last 3–5 letters are enough, for example `7K3QD`.
3. Check the items. **Always go by the admin page, not the WhatsApp text.** Customers can change the text.
4. If the customer saw a different price, the order shows a yellow warning. You can tap **Use customer's prices**.
5. Add the delivery charge (and a discount, if any). You may type the customer's name and city.
6. Tap **Confirm**. Stock goes down and it counts as a sale.
   If the money has already come, tap **Confirmed & paid** instead.
7. When money arrives later, tap **Mark paid** and choose UPI, cash, bank, card or other.
8. Then tap **Packed**, **Sent** (you can note the courier and tracking number) and **Delivered**.
   You may skip a step.

### Phone orders, farm-gate sales
Use **Orders → New order**. Pick the honey and pack. Save it as New, or as Confirmed.

### A website order is missing
Ask the customer to forward the WhatsApp message, or copy it. Go to **New order → Paste a WhatsApp message**.
The admin rebuilds the order from the message. If an order with that code already exists, it tells you first.

### Gift box and bulk requests
They appear on **Requests**, sorted by the date the customer needs them. After you reply on WhatsApp,
tick **Replied**.

### Invoice
On a confirmed order, open **Invoice**. Check it, then tap **Make the invoice**. The number (for example
`BZ/2026-27/0001`) is given only then, and never reused. Tap **Print**. A5 and A4 both work.
After that the order's items, prices, delivery charge and discount are fixed, and **Undo confirm** is gone.
If the order was wrong, cancel it and make a new one.

## 3. Harvest and stock

Stock is counted your way: **kg for each honey**, and **pieces** for candles and honey sticks.

### When you harvest
1. Go to **Harvests → Add harvest**.
2. Pick the honey, the date and the kg (for example 100).
3. Leave **Make a batch for labels and Know Your Jar** on. The batch code is filled in for you (for example `MUR-2611-01`).
   Best-before is set to 24 months after the harvest. You can change both.
4. Save. The stock goes up straight away. The batch can be found on the website's **Know Your Jar** page
   within about a minute, unless you switch "Show in Know Your Jar" off.

### Checking and correcting stock
- **Stock** shows what is left, for example "99.5 kg", and roughly how many jars that makes.
- If the real amount is different, open the honey and type what you really have today (**Count**).
  Earlier orders are never subtracted again.
- **Count all** lets you count everything in one go, check the differences, then save.
- For a broken jar, a sample or a return, open the honey and use **Something else changed**, then pick the reason.
- Red numbers mean the admin thinks you have less than zero. Count that honey soon. Orders are never blocked.

### Until you count a honey
A honey you have never harvested or counted shows "In stock" on the website, and gives no alerts.
The first harvest or count turns stock tracking on.

### Labels
**Labels** prints stickers for a batch: batch number, packed date, best before, MRP (incl. of all taxes), price per
100 g and the FSSAI number. The sheet is A4 with 65 stickers (38.1 × 21.2 mm) unless you choose another. On these small
stickers the QR code to Know Your Jar is off; you can switch it on. Skip stickers you already used.
Check the MRP, then tap **Save this MRP for the batch**: reprints of that batch use the same MRP, even after a price change.

## 4. Prices

1. Go to **Prices**. Every pack is in one list.
2. Change a price and tap **Save**. The website shows it within about a minute.
3. If a price changes by more than half, or goes below ₹10, the admin asks you to check. This catches typing
   ₹35 instead of ₹350.
4. Leave a price empty for "Price on WhatsApp".
5. Every change is in the price history. **Undo** puts the old price back.
6. To change many prices at once (for example +10%), use **Change many prices at once**. It shows a preview first and
   rounds to ₹5.

### Showing prices on the website
Prices stay hidden until you switch on **Website → Show prices on the website**. Do this only after your
accountant answers the GST and label questions. You can switch it off again at any time.

## 5. Sales

**Sales** shows money and kg sold for a month, a calendar year, a financial year (April–March) or any dates.
The arrows show the change from the period before. **By product** shows each honey and pack.
**Asked vs confirmed** compares website orders with real sales.
Only confirmed orders count. New, cancelled and "Not a real order" never count.

## 6. What each order word means

| Word | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **New** | The customer sent the order from the website (or you typed it). Stock has not changed yet. |
| **Confirmed** | You agreed the order with the customer. Stock went down and it counts as a sale. |
| **Packed** | Packed and ready to send. |
| **Sent** | Handed to the courier or the customer. |
| **Delivered** | The customer has it. |
| **Cancelled** | Not going ahead. Stock went back if it was confirmed. |
| **Not a real order** | A test or a mistake. It never counts. |

Payment is separate: **Not paid**, **Part paid** and **Paid** follow the payments you record.
A refund is a payment with a minus amount.

Undo and cancel:
- **Undo confirm** (while Confirmed, before an invoice is made) puts the stock back and makes the order New again.
- **Cancel order** from New changes nothing in stock. From Confirmed or Packed the stock goes back.
  From Sent or Delivered the admin asks "Did the jars come back to stock?".

## 7. The website

- **Website** holds the switches: Show prices, the delivery note, the gift box settings, the offer banner,
  your business details, and **Pause order recording** (use it only if strange orders flood in).
- **Offer banner**: type a short text, an optional page (for example `/gift-box/`) and a last day.
  It shows as a small banner above the WhatsApp button. Visitors can close it.
- **Products**: edit a honey's text and photos, add packs, or add a new product. **Save** on a product that is
  on the website also updates the website.
- Photos: tap the upload tile and choose a photo from your phone. The admin makes it the right size and removes the
  hidden location data.
- The chip at the top shows the website state: "Website up to date ✓", "Updating…" or "Changes waiting".
  An update takes about 2–5 minutes (up to about 8). If it says **"Update didn't finish"**, your website is safe and
  shows the previous version. Go to **Website** and tap **Update website now**. If it fails twice, call your helper.

## 8. Once a month

Go to **Backup → Download everything now**. Save the file to Google Drive or a pen drive.
The admin also makes an automatic backup every night.

---

## 9. If something looks wrong

1. Open **Help → Self-check**. Green is fine. Red tells you what is wrong.
2. Your public website keeps working even when the admin has a problem. Customers can still order on WhatsApp.
3. If an order is missing in the admin, use **Paste a WhatsApp message**.
4. If stock looks wrong, **Count** that honey.
5. If a price looks wrong, open **Prices → history** and tap **Undo**.
6. If you cannot log in: check spam, wait a minute and ask for a new code, or use a recovery code.
   After too many tries the admin asks you to wait.
7. Every change is listed on **Activity**, with who made it and when.
8. Still stuck? Call your helper. Do not share your login codes with anyone.
